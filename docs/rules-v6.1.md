**GLADIATOR**

*A Card Game for 2-6 Players*

V6.1

**Welcome to the arena (Overview)**

Command your Gladiator, equip them with weapons and armor, then attack
other Gladiators with your cards and dice. With a little luck, you'll
win an arena by being the last Gladiator standing.

Becoming the champion requires luck and strategy to obtain the best
weapons to face off your opponents. Buy them from the Market or try your
luck by drawing from the deck.

**Winning the Game**

Win the arena by being the last Gladiator standing. Each arena win earns
that player a laurel, and the first player to claim 3 laurels is crowned
Champion of the Arena and wins the game.

**Setup**

1.  Shuffle the Gladiator, Equipment, Attack and Hades decks. Place them
    face-down in the middle of the table where all players can reach. If
    any deck runs out, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.

2.  Flip **3 cards** from the equipment deck face-up and set them up in
    the middle of the table. This is the Market (Mercatus) where you
    will buy and sell Equipment.

3.  Set the denarii in the middle of the table to form the Bank
    (Argentarius).

4.  Each player draws a Gladiator card and places it on the table
    face-up. Once per arena, after all players have drawn, you can send
    your Gladiator back and draw a new one. Returned gladiators do not
    play in this arena, discard them.

5.  Each player gets a health tracker, a six-sided die, a red cube to
    track health (VITA), and a gold cube to track their laurels. Set the
    red cube at your Gladiator's starting health and the gold cube at 0
    laurels.

6.  Place the Sacred Coin in the middle of the table for players to use.

7.  Each players rolls their die for turn order. If players tie, those
    players reroll.

**Turn Structure**

An arena has several turns, and each turn has two phases: the Equip
Phase and the Attack Phase.

**Phase 1 --- Equip Phase**

On your turn, take the following actions as part of the Equip Phase:

**1 --- Income**

Collect 30 denarii from the Bank (Argentarius). You collect 30 denarii
every turn, including your first. Unspent denarii carry over between
your turns, but reset to 0 at the start of each new arena.

**2 --- Sell (optional)**

You may sell any number of cards from your hand or cards equipped to
your Gladiator at any point during your Equip Phase. Each card sells for
50% of its buy price, as printed on each card. Sold cards are placed in
the discard pile.

**3 --- Refresh the Market (optional)**

Once per phase, before drawing or buying cards, you may pay 20 denarii
to discard all 3 equipment cards in the Market and replace them with 3
new ones.

**4 --- Draw or Buy**

Draw 1 card for free from the Equipment deck OR Buy up to 2 cards from
the Market. After each purchase, immediately refill the Market to 3
cards.

**5 --- Equip your Gladiator**

Gladiators start the game unarmed until you equip them with a weapon. To
Equip an item (weapon, armor, etc), move it from your hand to the table
alongside your Gladiator.

During the Equip phase, you may freely move any number of cards between
your hand and your Gladiator, as many times as you like. Nothing is
committed until you finish. Once you complete the Equip Phase, your
Gladiator's equipment is locked until your next Equip Phase, you cannot
swap weapons or armor mid-combat. The cards in your hand are not
equipped.

When all players have completed their Equip phase, proceed to the Attack
phase.

**Phase 2 --- Attack Phase**

In turn order, each player with a surviving Gladiator draws one Attack
Card and attacks the designated opponent. If your Gladiator is dead,
draw a Hades card and resolve it.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Attack Card**               **Effect**
  ----------------------------- -----------------------------------------
  Attack Left                   Attack the player to your left.

  Attack Right                  Attack the player to your right.

  Attack Your Choice            Attack a player of your choice.

  Pass                          Do nothing this Attack Phase.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

In a 2-player game, do not draw Attack Cards. Each Gladiator attacks the
other Gladiator each Attack Phase. (Fulgur Iovis simply attacks your
single opponent once.)

**Attacking**

**Warning:** If your Gladiator has not been equipped with a weapon, they
will fight with their fists only (a base attack of 10).

First, see if you hit your opponent's Gladiator. Roll 1 die and compare
it to their Gladiator's Dodge range. Note that each Gladiator has a base
Dodge range, but things they Equip can modify their Dodge range. If your
roll is higher than the opponent's Dodge range, you will score a hit.

**Important: a Gladiator\'s dodge range can never exceed 4 or drop below
1, even with modifiers.**

To determine how much damage your attack does, add up the attack points
of all of your weapons then subtract the total armor your opponent's
Gladiator has, your opponent then subtracts that damage from their
health by using the tracker.

If using a beast (Leo, Ursus, Canis or Cerberus), you will need to roll
again to determine the damage they inflict.

**If you roll a 6**: it's an auto-hit! Add up the attack points of all
of your weapons, your opponent takes that damage. Ignore the armor on
your opponent's Gladiator. Beasts still roll for damage.

**Example: doing damage**

Let's say you have a Vulgaris Gladiator and your opponent has a Velox.
You roll a 5, their dodge is 2, you are hitting them.

In this example, you are equipped with a Gladius (50 attack) and they
are equipped with a subarmalis (padded vest, 20 defense). The damage
would be 50 attack -- 20 defense = 30 damage.

If you had rolled a 2 or less the opponent's Gladiator would have
dodged, and you would have done 0 damage.

If you had rolled a 6, the auto-hit would do 50 damage, since the
subarmalis would be ignored.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Roll**               **Outcome**
  ---------------------- ------------------------------------------------
  Equal to or less than  Miss. No damage.
  Target's dodge         

  Higher than Target's   Regular hit. Damage = weapon ATK − armor,
  dodge                  minimum 5. Round up to the nearest 5.

  6                      Auto-hit. Attacks ignore armor (round up to the
                         nearest 5).
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Example: Stacking Modifiers**

Aurelia plays the Invictus Gladiator (dodges on 3) and has drawn the
legendary Talaria Mercurii (Sandals of Mercury), which adds 2 to her
dodge. Her dodge would become 5, but the dodge cap is 4. Her modified
dodge range becomes **4**.

Marcus attacks with a Rete (a net) equipped, which shrinks Aurelia\'s
dodge by 1. Her final dodge is **3** for this attack.

**Death: The Emperor's Favor**

When your Gladiator reaches 0 VITA for the first time, flip the Sacred
Coin.

-   Heads, the Emperor bestows their favor, sends medics to the arena,
    and the Gladiator is revived with 30 VITA (update the health
    tracker). The Gladiator is back in the game! If they have not
    attacked, they may attack.

-   Tails, the Emperor was not impressed. The Gladiator is removed from
    the arena as the crowd boos.

The coin can only be flipped once per Gladiator per arena. If a
Gladiator reaches 0 VITA for a second time, they are dead and are on
their way to Hades.

**Furor Mortis**

When your Gladiator is eliminated, will they take revenge on their
killer? Flip the Sacred Coin once more before they take their trip to
Hades:\
• Heads: with their dying breath, they make one final attack against the
Gladiator that killed them, following the usual attack rules.\
• Tails: no final attack. They board the boat to cross into Hades.

No final attack if the Gladiator was killed by a Hades card. A final
attack can\'t trigger another final attack. If no Gladiator is left
standing, no one earns a laurel.

**Hades**

When a Gladiator is eliminated, they draw from the Hades deck for the
rest of the arena, and can still meddle with the living Gladiators from
the underworld.

On your turn in the Attack Phase, draw one Hades card and resolve it as
your attack, then discard it.

Hades damage ignores armor and can kill a Gladiator; the Sacred Coin
flip applies as usual. If a Gladiator can\'t pay a denarii penalty in
full, they pay what they have.

**End of turn / end the arena**

The turn ends after all living Gladiators have attacked and all dead
Gladiators have resolved their Hades cards. If more than one Gladiator
is still alive, start another turn (equip then attack).

The arena ends when only one Gladiator is still alive, they receive a
laurel as their prize.

When a new arena begins, a new crop of Gladiators enters the arena and
the game resets. Put back all Gladiator and equipment cards and
reshuffle. Return all denarii to the bank. Reset all decks and discard
piles.
